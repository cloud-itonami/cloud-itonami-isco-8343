# Business Model: Independent Crane & Hoist Operations Practice

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-8343`

- Social impact: worker-safety, local-jobs

## Customer

- general contractors
- construction firms

## Offer

- crane rental with operator
- rigging services
- lift planning

## Revenue

- per-lift fee
- day-rate contract

## Trust Controls

- no lift above the crane's registered load-capacity ceiling without governor gate
- pre-lift inspection must pass before any lift is dispatched
- lift and inspection records are auditable, not editable
